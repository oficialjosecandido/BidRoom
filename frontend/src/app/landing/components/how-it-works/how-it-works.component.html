How BidRoom Works

Everything you need to know to start bidding and winning on BidRoom

01

User Authentication

Get started quickly and securely with BidRoom

🔐
Secure Sign Up
1️⃣

Create Your Account

Sign up for free using your email address or Google account. Registration takes less than a minute.

  • Free registration - no credit card required
  • Sign up with email or Google
  • Strong password policy for security
2️⃣

Verify Your Email

Check your inbox for a verification email. You must verify your email address before you can bid or list items.

  • Email verification required for security
  • Prevents unauthorized account access
  • Resend verification email if needed
3️⃣

Start Bidding

Once verified, you can immediately start placing bids on auctions. No additional setup required.

  • Place bids instantly after verification
  • Guest bidding available with email only
  • Complete profile optional for better experience
02

Listing Your Items

Sell your items in 4 simple steps

📦
List & Sell
1️⃣

Item Details & Format

Enter your item's title, category, condition, and choose between Auction or Best Offer format.

  • Descriptive title (up to 80 characters)
  • Select category and sub-category
  • Choose auction type (Highest Bid or Best Offer)
  • Set item condition (New, Used, etc.)
2️⃣

Media & Description

Upload at least 3 high-quality photos and write a detailed description to attract buyers.

  • Upload multiple images (minimum 3 required)
  • Add detailed description (minimum 50 characters)
  • Include item specifications and dimensions
  • Specify item location for shipping
3️⃣

Pricing & Private Room

Set your starting price, reserve price, and choose whether to enable the Private Auction Room.

  • Set starting bid and optional reserve price
  • Choose auction duration (2 hours to 7 days)
  • Optional Buy Now price for instant purchase
  • Enable Private Room for 2.0% commission (or 0.5% without)
4️⃣

Shipping & Review

Configure shipping options, handling time, return policy, and review the fee summary before listing.

  • Select shipping method (flat rate or calculated)
  • Set handling time (days to prepare item)
  • Choose return policy
  • Review commission fees before confirming
03

Private Auction Room

An exclusive bidding experience for select bidders

🔒
Exclusive Access
🏁

Auction Ends & Invitation Window

When the main auction ends, if Private Room is enabled, platinum bidders receive invitations with a 5-minute acceptance window.

  • Main auction closes normally
  • Up to 5 platinum bidders are selected
  • Invitations sent via email immediately
  • 5-minute window to accept invitation
⏱️

Acceptance Window

Platinum bidders have 5 minutes to accept their invitation. The private room starts automatically after the window expires if at least one bidder accepted.

  • 5-minute acceptance window after auction ends
  • Bidders can accept or decline invitation
  • If no one accepts, highest bidder wins automatically
  • Private room activates if at least one accepts
🎰

Private Room Bidding

The private auction room opens with a poker table-style interface. Only invited platinum bidders can participate in this exclusive round.

  • Exclusive access for platinum bidders only
  • Starts with highest bid from main auction
  • Real-time poker table view
  • Soft closing: each bid extends deadline by 30 seconds
⏰

Soft Closing Mechanism

Each new bid extends the auction deadline by 30 seconds. If no bid is placed within 30 seconds, the auction closes automatically.

  • 30-second countdown timer resets with each bid
  • Auction closes when countdown reaches zero
  • Winning bidder is determined automatically
  • Seller must honor sale if reserve price is met
💰

Commission Structure

When you enable Private Room at listing creation, you commit to a 2.0% commission rate (instead of 0.5%). This applies regardless of whether the private room is triggered.

  • 2.0% commission if Private Room is enabled
  • 0.5% commission if Private Room is disabled
  • Commission applies whether private room triggers or not
  • Clear fee summary shown before listing
