How Payouts Work

When a buyer pays for your won auction:

  1. Buyer is charged: Item Price + 2% BidRoom fee + Stripe processing fee
  2. BidRoom retains its 2% service fee
  3. Stripe deducts its processing fee (2.9% + $0.30)
  4. You receive: Item Price minus Stripe's processing fee
