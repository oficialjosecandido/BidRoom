Fee Breakdown Guide

BidRoom Fee (2%)

Service fee charged to the buyer. Applied to the winning bid amount.

Stripe Processing Fee (2.9% + $0.30)

Payment processing fee charged by Stripe. Deducted from the total before seller payout.

Seller Payout

Amount the seller receives: Item Price minus Stripe's processing fee.
